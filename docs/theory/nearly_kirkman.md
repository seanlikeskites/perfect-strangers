# Nearly Kirkman Triple Matching
A Nearly Kirkman Triple System is a <a style="text-decoration: none;"
href="./block_designs#group-divisible-designs">$\left(3, 1\right)$-$\mathrm{RGDD}$ of type $2^{n}$</a>. Baker and Wilson
(1977)[^1] showed that such systems exists where the total number of points is greater than or equal to 18 and is divisible
by 6. This means that $l_{\max}(\alpha, 3)$ is equal to the [trivial upper bound](./overview.md#trivial-upper-bound) when
$\alpha \geq 6$ and is even. The non existence of systems for fewer points also implies that $l_{\max}(4, 3)$ is less than
the trivial upper bound.

## Constructions
The perfect-strangers package currently implements the nearly Kirkman triple systems given in Table I of [Abel et al.
(2013)](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcd.21342).

[^1]: Baker, R.D. and Wilson, R.M., 1977. Nearly Kirkman triple systems. Utilitas Math, 11, pp.289-296.
